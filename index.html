      How the checks work

      Each file type gets its own set of signal checks. Results are combined into one verdict.

      1

      Images

      Re-compression consistency finds regions edited after capture, noise texture flags over-smooth AI output, and file metadata reveals generator or editor tags.

      2

      Video

      Frames are sampled across the clip and checked like images. Flicker in texture between frames is a common sign of face-swaps and re-rendering.

      3

      Audio

      Background noise floor, spectral variation, high-frequency content, and abrupt edits are drawn on a spectrogram to expose synthetic or spliced speech.

      Questions people ask

      Straight answers, no jargon.

      Is my file uploaded anywhere?

      No. Everything runs in your browser. Your history is saved only on this device and you can clear it any time.

      What do the three verdicts mean?

      Likely Authentic means no strong signs of tampering. AI-Generated means the file has patterns typical of synthetic creation. Manipulated means real media that looks edited, spliced, or swapped.

      How accurate is it?

      This prototype uses transparent signal checks and gives estimates, not proof. Strong results are a good reason to look closer. Trained face-swap and voice-clone models are the next upgrade.

      Why was my real photo flagged?

      Screenshots, filters, and social-media re-saves strip camera data and add edit-like traces. Try the original file straight from the camera.

      Which files work?

      Images (JPG, PNG, WebP), video (MP4, WebM), and audio (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG) up to 150 MB. Audio analysis covers the first 60 seconds.

      What should I do with a suspicious result?

      Don’t share it yet. Find the original source, check whether trusted outlets have it, and ask the sender where it came from.

      Veridex is a working prototype. It uses transparent signal analysis, not a trained deepfake model, so results are estimates to guide review and not proof. Face-swap, lip-sync, and voice-clone classifiers are the next modules to add. Always confirm important findings with a trusted source.
